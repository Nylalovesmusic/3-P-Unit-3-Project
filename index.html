Bacon Egg And Cheese on a Roll

  • Time: _
  • Serves: _
Ingredients Quantity
Bacon Minimum 1 Maximum infinty
Eggs 2
American cheese 1 to 2 slices
Bread roll 2 slices
Salt and pepper A handful
Butter As much as you want

Step 1: Prepare The Bread.

Place the bread on the counter and spread butter on top.

Then put the buttered bread on the stove at medium heat.

Source: Some recipes websites

Source: How to make a bec

Step 2: Prepare The Bacon

The bacon should be cooked with butter on a stove at medium heat

Cook until crispy

Step 3: Prepare The Eggs

Cook the eggs on a stove on medium heat

Cook them either scrambled or over easy.

Add salt and pepper to eggs

Place your cheese on top of the eggs until it melts

Step 3: Assemble The Sandwich

Place the cooked bacon and eggs on the bread
